## Entry

> One star. Drifting away 3.8 cm a year — the slowest breakup in history, and it won't even text back.

100 characters. A real review voice, a grievance only the Moon could earn — it recedes 3.8 cm from Earth every year — and the punchline lands before the limit does. No craters, no cheese, no brightness jokes.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies, no network) writes the review to `/output/entry.txt`, where the bounty gate scorer reads it:

